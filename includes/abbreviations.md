*[Divine Spark]: The all-powerful source of life energy — in truth an individual who Ascended so high they became a creator god.
*[Ascension]: The process by which elves evolve into higher tiers of being through deeper attunement with the Divine Spark.
*[Spark Weaving]: Elven magic — the channelling of Spark, the raw life energy of the Divine Spark.
*[Magi]: Elven spell-casters trained in Spark Weaving.
*[Calaquendi]: The elven elite — a privileged upper class, represented as a pop and an estate in the EU5 mod.
*[Aeluran]: The faith and religious Order devoted to the Divine Spark.
*[Grand Portal]: The gateway linking Earth to the Divine Spark's network of connected worlds.
*[Mythril]: The legendary elven metal.
*[Ainur]: The divine order — Maiar, Valar and Aratar — the highest servants of the Divine Spark.
*[Valar]: The pantheon of mighty gods of the Divine Spark; elves who Ascended to the eleventh tier.
*[Maia]: A demi-god servant of the Valar; the tenth Ascension tier and lowest rank of the Ainur.
*[Maiar]: Demi-god servants of the Valar; the tenth Ascension tier and lowest rank of the Ainur.
*[Aratar]: The mightiest few among the Valar; the twelfth and highest Ascension tier.
*[Eldar]: The ninth Ascension tier in the CK3 mod — the same tier the EU5 mod names Vanyar.
*[Vanyar]: The ninth Ascension tier in the EU5 mod — the same tier the CK3 mod names Eldar.
*[Melkor]: The fallen god of the Valar pantheon.
