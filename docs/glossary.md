# Glossary

A quick reference to the recurring terms of Elf Destiny. Most of these terms also
appear as hover tooltips throughout the wiki — look for the dotted underline.

## A

**Aeluran** — The faith devoted to the [Divine Spark](lore/divine-spark-and-cosmology.md),
and the religious **Order** that administers it. See
[The Aeluran Religion & Order](lore/aeluran-religion-and-order.md).

**Ainur** — The divine order of gods of the elves: the [Maiar](#m), [Valar](#v)
and [Aratar](#a), the highest servants of the Divine Spark.

**Aratar** — The mightiest few among the Valar; the twelfth and highest tier of
[Ascension](lore/elven-ascension.md).

**Ascension** — The process by which elves evolve into higher tiers of being
through ever-deeper attunement with the Divine Spark. See
[Elven Ascension](lore/elven-ascension.md).

## C

**Calaquendi** — The elven elite: a privileged upper class. In the EU5 mod they
are both a pop type and an [estate](eu5/estates-privileges.md).

**Celestial** — The seventh tier of Ascension, where true, permanent immortality
begins.

## D

**Dark Elves** — Elves who pursue power through blood magic and the [Void](#v).
See [Dark Elves](lore/dark-elves.md).

**Divine Spark** — The all-powerful source of life energy for the connected
worlds. In truth, an individual who Ascended so extraordinarily high that they
became a creator god. See [The Divine Spark & Cosmology](lore/divine-spark-and-cosmology.md).

## E

**Eldar** — The ninth tier of Ascension in the **CK3** mod — the same tier the
EU5 mod names [Vanyar](#v).

## F

**Fae** — The fifth tier of Ascension; the first of the regal Fae forms that
stand above the common elf.

## G

**Grand Portal** — The gateway linking Earth to the Divine Spark's network of
connected worlds. Restoring it is the central goal of both mods. See
[The Portal Network](lore/portal-network.md).

## M

**Magi** — Elven spell-casters trained in [Spark Weaving](#s).

**Maia** *(plural Maiar)* — A demi-god servant of the Valar; the tenth tier of
Ascension and the lowest rank of the Ainur.

**Melkor** — The fallen god of the [Valar](#v) pantheon. See
[The Valar Pantheon](lore/valar-pantheon.md).

**Mythril** — The legendary elven metal, used for coinage and the finest arms
and armour.

## S

**Spark** — The raw life energy the Divine Spark creates, which attuned elves
channel to work magic, Ascend, and live their long lives.

**Spark Weaving** — Elven magic: the disciplined channelling of Spark. See
[Magic](lore/magic.md).

## V

**Vala** *(plural Valar)* — A mighty god of the Divine Spark; the eleventh tier
of Ascension. The Valar are simply elves who Ascended that far. See
[The Valar Pantheon](lore/valar-pantheon.md).

**Vanyar** — The ninth tier of Ascension in the **EU5** mod — the same tier the
CK3 mod names [Eldar](#e).

**Void** — The shadowy realm between the connected worlds, and the prison into
which the world's worst horrors were once banished.
