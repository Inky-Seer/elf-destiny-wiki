# Estates & Privileges

Elven society is governed through its estates — and Elf Destiny adds a new one, the
**Calaquendi**, alongside fresh privileges for both the Calaquendi and the Aeluran
clergy.

## The Calaquendi estate

The **Calaquendi** are the high nobility of the elves — the elite above the elite.
The mod adds them as a full estate, with their own [pop type](pops-cultures.md#the-calaquendi),
their own privileges, and their own influence in the [Elven Parliament](parliament.md).
Any country with an elven culture gains access to them.

## Calaquendi privileges

The Calaquendi estate offers nine privileges. Granting them empowers the elven
elite — generally pushing society toward aristocracy and quality:

| Privilege | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Elven High Councils** | Lets the Calaquendi sit in parliament, with greater agenda impact and cabinet efficiency. |
| **Calaquendi Apprenticeships** | Unlocks expensive education for elven children and raises noble and Calaquendi literacy. |
| **Elite Worship** | The Calaquendi are revered almost as demi-gods — boosts control and suppresses rebellion, at the clergy's expense. |
| **Personal Dominions** | Grants the Calaquendi their own autonomous lands to rule. |
| **Personal Retinues** | The Calaquendi maintain their own elite regiments — bonus discipline and levy quality. |
| **War Masters** | Hands the Calaquendi authority over war, barring other estates from leading armies. |
| **State-Sponsored Ascendancy** | Diverts treasury funds to the Calaquendi's great works — religious influence and magi power, at a tax cost. |
| **Hierarchical Authority** | Cements Calaquendi dominance, sidelining the lesser estates from cabinet and agenda. |
| **Fairest Ascendancy** | *(Requires the Beguiling Nature aspect.)* Brings gender equality, diplomatic might, and a unique succession. |

## Aeluran privileges

When an elven realm follows the Aeluran faith, the **clergy estate** becomes the
Aeluran Order, and gains four privileges of its own:

| Privilege | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Aeluran Advisors** | An Aeluran sister takes a seat in your cabinet — bonus cabinet efficiency, religious influence, and a female cabinet. |
| **Daughters of the Red** | The Order may take magically talented daughters away to be trained — rulers cannot refuse. |
| **Aeluran Judiciary** | Aeluran sisters serve as the judges of your realm — stability and legitimacy, at the crown's expense. |
| **Aeluran Etiquette** | The realm is steeped in Aeluran custom — greatly faster assimilation and conversion, and a powerful clergy. |

Together, the two privilege sets are the levers of elven politics: how much power
you hand the magical elite, and how deep you let the sisterhood into your
government.
