# Pops & Cultures

Elf Destiny adds elven cultures to the map and, above the ordinary nobility, an
entirely new pop type — the **Calaquendi**.

## Elven cultures

The mod adds seven elven cultures, all part of the **elven culture group** and all
speaking the same elven tongue, the Sindarin dialect:

| Culture | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Elven** | The generic elven culture. |
| **Deepwood Elven** | The heartland culture of the northern elven realms. |
| **Alfar** | The martial northern elves; also tied to the Scandinavian group. |
| **Romaviri** | The Mediterranean elves, tied to the Italian and Greek groups. |
| **Astrion** | The scholarly, magical elves. |
| **Tuatha de Danann** | The Celtic elves — kindred with Irish culture. |
| **Atlareth** | The seafaring Celtic-Breton elves — on good terms with the Bretons. |

Every elven culture shares one defining trait: **elves do not migrate**. Peasant
migration is switched off for them — an elven people stays rooted to its homeland.

For the deeper background of these cultures, see the lore section's
[Elven Cultures](../lore/elven-cultures.md) page.

## The Calaquendi

The headline addition to elven society is the **Calaquendi** — *"the true elites of
elven society, the nobles amongst nobles. They are the wisest and most powerful
few."*

The Calaquendi are both a **pop type** and an [estate](estates-privileges.md). As a
pop type they are:

- **The upper crust** — an upper-class pop that sits even above ordinary nobles.
- **Rare by nature** — they do not grow on their own; their numbers are capped, and
  new Calaquendi are **promoted up from the nobility** rather than born into the
  role wholesale.
- **Quietly powerful** — their presence lifts a location's control, and they form
  the population base for the Calaquendi estate and its [High Magi levies](advances-traits-units.md).

Any country with an elven culture unlocks the Calaquendi pop and estate. Growing
your Calaquendi population is a long-term investment: they are few, but they are the
foundation of elven magical and military power.
