# Europa Universalis V Mod

**Elf Destiny** for Europa Universalis V brings the world's premier elven Crusader
Kings III mod into the age of EU5 — a newer, streamlined take on the same fantasy
world.

## The premise

At the beginning of civilisation, elves and humans came to this world through a
portal from another realm. Countless empires have risen and fallen since, and the
elves have faded to the edges of the world and memory. You are the new **Elf Lord**,
and your task is to restore the greatness of the elves — and to decide what this new
elven people will be, and how they will treat the humans among them.

## What the mod adds

| System | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Elves & Ascension** | A racial trait granting pointed ears, long life, and an ageless look — and a twelve-tier Ascension ladder climbing toward godhood. |
| **The Calaquendi** | A new pop type *and* estate — the nobles among nobles, the true elite of elven society. |
| **The Aeluran faith** | A new elven religion built on worship of the Divine Spark, with religious influence, holy sites, and Blessings. |
| **The Elven institution** | A new institution with its own advance tree — crafting, nature, magic, and war. |
| **The Portal Network** | The restored Grand Portal and the teleport network it powers. |
| **Government & estates** | Elven government reforms, the Elven Parliament, and new estate privileges. |
| **Genetics** | An inherited-trait system that makes royal marriages a real decision. |

It also adds new countries, units, buildings, trade goods (including **Mythril**),
and game-rule options worth checking before you start.

## Getting started

1. **Subscribe** on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3681153070).
2. Enable the mod and start a campaign. Shortly after the game begins, an
   **archaeologist** visits your realm with word of ancient elven ruins and a
   request for funding.
3. Help her, and her return launches the quest to restore the elven world — and
   unlocks the **Fund Expedition** interaction. Soon after, your characters can be
   **transformed into elves**.

!!! info "Beta software"
    Elf Destiny for EU5 is a fresh, actively developed mod. Its story content is
    roughly in line with the CK3 version and grows alongside it. Expect balance
    rough edges — bug reports and feedback are very welcome on the Discord.
