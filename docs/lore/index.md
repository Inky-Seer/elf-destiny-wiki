# Lore

The shared world of Elf Destiny — its history, peoples, magic, and gods — told the
same across both the Crusader Kings III and Europa Universalis V mods.

Elves once ruled this world. They came through a great portal in the First Age,
raised empires on every continent, and were cast down by a human rebellion that
scattered them to the margins of the earth. What follows is the world they left
behind, and the one they may yet reclaim.
