# The Portal Network

The Grand Portal is an ancient interdimensional gateway that once connected this
world to the wider network of realms across the galaxy. It is the single most
important structure in the world of Elf Destiny — and the heart of the main story.

## What is the Grand Portal?

In ancient times, both Elves and Humans first came to this world *through* the Grand
Portal. Empires rose and fell. The Elven emperor was eventually overthrown in a
massive rebellion, and most Elves were slain or forced to flee back through the
portal — an event remembered as the **Great Exodus**. A small number stayed behind,
living quietly in the wilds.

As the Elves fled, the rebels destroyed the portal's vital components. The gateway
to the other realms went dark, and the way home was lost.

> *"In ancient times, Elves and Humans came here through a great portal... After the
> Great Exodus, rebels destroyed vital components of the portal and our gateway to
> the other realms was lost."*
> — The High Matriarch

The portal itself is less a doorway than a place: a vast magical labyrinth running
beneath the earth, which one travels through to emerge on the far side of the world
— or far beyond it.

## The portal at game start

The portal's **vital components** were destroyed, but its ruined structure still
survives, hidden somewhere in the world. At the start of a campaign it has not yet
been found. Restoring it is the spine of the main questline.

??? warning "Main story spoilers — the restoration quest"
    The questline to restore the Grand Portal runs roughly as follows:

    1. An archaeological expedition discovers the portal ruins.
    2. The High Matriarch reveals their history and sets the mission:
       *"The world's fate lies in your hands. I do not say that lightly."*
    3. Further expeditions hunt down the replacement components — the
       **Spark Capacitor** and the **Navigation Relay**.
    4. With the components gathered, the portal can finally be repaired.

    > *"After many trials and great personal cost the Grand Portal is finally
    > restored! Once more the gateway between this world and the countless realms of
    > the galaxy is open... The time of Elves comes again!"*

    Completing the restoration is the current end of the implemented main story.

## What restoration unlocks

Once the Grand Portal is whole again, the portal network becomes a working part of
your realm:

- **Portal Hub** — the primary network station, built at the Grand Portal site.
- **Lesser Portal Nodes** — secondary connection points in other locations.
- **Teleport Army** — move armies instantly between connected portal locations.
- **Portal Treaties** — agreements that let friendly nations exchange population and
  goods through the portal.

## Why it matters for the world

Reopening the portal does more than move armies around:

- **Ascension goes further.** Without the portal there is not enough latent Spark
  energy in the world to sustain Ascension beyond the True Elf tier. A restored
  portal refills that well.
- **The Aelurans take notice.** The Aeluran order is an inter-realm organization.
  With the portal open, higher-ranking and far more powerful sisters begin to
  arrive — bearing demands, and "gifts".
- **You are watched.** An open gateway is an open invitation. Restoring the portal
  may eventually draw the attention of powers from beyond — not all of them friendly.

## The Realm Lord

Before restoration, the chosen player carries the title of **Realm Lord**. Once the
Grand Portal is restored, this becomes **True Realm Lord** — a lasting recognition of
the player's worthiness, and a signal that the time of Elves has come again.
