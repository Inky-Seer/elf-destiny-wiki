# The Aeluran Religion & Order

All elves worship the [Divine Spark](divine-spark-and-cosmology.md). The **Aeluran
faith** is the formal tradition through which that worship is practised — and it is
inseparable from the **Aeluran Order**, the sisterhood that has kept it alive.

## The faith

The Aeluran faith venerates the Divine Spark as the source of all life. Its
spiritual head is the **High Matriarch** of the Aeluran Order, and its holiest sites
are the ruins of the Grand Portal — and, once it is rebuilt, the
[restored Portal](portal-network.md) itself.

The faith holds bloodline above taboo: close marriage is no sin where the purity of
an elven line is at stake, and witchcraft is counted a virtue rather than a crime.
Its clergy are women, and women only.

## The Aeluran Order

Formally the **Order of the Aeluran Weavers** — and known to common folk, warily, as
the **Red Witches**.

> *Forgers of bloodlines and meddlers of fate, the Aeluran Weavers are a powerful
> force in this world. They act as wise advisors and matchmakers between the most
> powerful rulers in a relentless pursuit to breed optimal leaders for the elven
> peoples of the world.*

The Order is **entirely female**, and its single purpose is the prosperity of
elvenkind — achieved by placing worthy, capable leaders into power and shaping the
bloodlines that produce them. It is customary for an elf ruler to grant the Aeluran
Sisters some measure of authority over the running of their realm.

### Ranks of the Order

| Rank | Description |
|---|---|
| **Servant** | Novices in training. |
| **Aeluran Sister** | Trained from youth in the ways of the Order — moulded into powerful magi and wise advisors. |
| **Aeluran Matron** | The most elite of the Order, forming its leadership council; far more gifted with the Spark than common sisters. |
| **High Matriarch** | The undisputed leader of the Aelurans, chosen by the Council of Matrons — typically the wisest and most powerful sister of all. |

Above even the Matrons sits the **Coven of Seven** — seven witches of unrivalled
mastery over the magic of the Divine Spark. Certain rituals can be performed by no
one but them.

Children born within the Order are said to be **"Born in the Red"** — marked from
birth as the sisterhood's hope and held to extraordinary standards.

### The Order in power

The Aeluran Order does not merely advise — it governs. An **Aeluran Advisor**,
appointed by the High Matriarch herself, sits in elven rulers' cabinets, and no
ruler may dismiss her; only the High Matriarch holds that authority.

Where the Order's grip is firmest, it becomes a government in its own right — a
dual-leadership structure, led exclusively by Aeluran Sisters, that rules a realm
directly rather than merely guiding it. The Order also fields its own battle-magi,
available only to those who serve it or keep an Aeluran Advisor at court.

The Red Sisters enforce religious law with a system of penances — chastity belts for
fornicators and deviants, walks of shame and lashing for murderers and cannibals.

### Matron's Hollow

In ancient days the Order kept a sacred retreat called **Matron's Hollow** — a place
dense in Spark and rich in tradition, where former Matriarchs would settle in their
later years to pass wisdom to the rising generation. The Order abandoned it long
ago, for reasons unknown, and it lies now among the explorable ruins of the world.

## Blessings

With enough religious devotion, an elven ruler can perform a ritual to convene with
the Divine Spark and receive a **Blessing** — a boon that falls upon the entire
elven population of their realm.

> *Blessings are bestowed upon a country by the Divine Spark as a show of its
> favour. These rewards can be powerful — if sometimes quite peculiar.*

The Spark works in mysterious ways, and its gifts can be strange. Some quicken a
people's fertility or fill their warriors with fearless courage; others lend an
effortless, magical charm, or — like the **Mythril Blessing** — cause a fresh
deposit of legendary metal to surface in a realm's mountains. Blessings named for
individual [Valar](valar-pantheon.md) carry something of that god's domain. The
restoration of the Grand Portal is itself rewarded with a free Blessing.

!!! note "What the future may hold"
    The Aeluran Order is destined to grow. With the Grand Portal restored, it is
    meant to reveal itself as an *inter-realm* organisation — higher and far more
    powerful sisters arriving from beyond to make demands and offer gifts — and lore
    points toward a possible future in which the sisterhood becomes a conquering
    power in its own right.

## How it plays in each game

=== "Crusader Kings III"

    The Aeluran faith installs a powerful **Aeluran Advisor** in place of the court
    chaplain — one no ruler may dismiss. Granting the Order authority drives the
    **Aeluran Realm Authority** law and the **Aeluran Regency**, and at its fullest
    extent the Order becomes a government of its own.

=== "Europa Universalis V"

    influence pays for [Ascension](elven-ascension.md) and **Blessings**.
