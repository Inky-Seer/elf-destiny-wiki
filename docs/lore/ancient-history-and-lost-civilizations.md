# Ancient History & Lost Civilizations

Elven history on Earth is vast, ancient, and mostly lost. What survives, survives as
**ruins** — the holy sites and fallen cities scattered across every continent, each
one the grave of a kingdom, a hero, or a catastrophe.

## The deep timeline

- **The Elder Ages** — the collective name for the earliest span of elven
  civilization, beginning with the First Age and encompassing many ages of early
  history. Most of what happened in them is lost.
  - **The First Age** — the opening chapter: when elves and humans first came through
    the Grand Portal, roughly 300,000 years ago. The stone city around the portal
    dates to this age.
- **The Grand Wars** — at least three world-shaking conflicts; one armoury is still
  remembered as the *Sealed Armoury of the Third Grand War*.
- **Countless empires** — elven civilization was once global, and empires beyond
  number rose and fell across it.
- **The Great Rebellion** — the human uprising that overthrew the Last Emperor and
  ended unified elven rule. (See [World History](world-history.md).)

One theme recurs through all of it: **most lost elven civilizations fell to their
own magical hubris** — not to invaders, but to their own runaway power.

## Northern & Eastern Europe

- **Ruins of Valfuria** — once the capital of a mighty Elf Empire, much of it eerily
  intact; something sinister is said to prowl its streets.
- **Fortress Tomb of the Last Emperor** — the final refuge of the last Elf Emperor,
  who withdrew here after his betrayal and defeat in the Great Rebellion.
- **The Proving Fields** — the ancient barracks of a famed martial order, where the
  greatest warriors of the world once gathered.
- **The Noble Wood** — a forest of titanic trees, the realm of a powerful elf queen,
  *"beautiful and terrible as the Morning and the Night."*
- **High Harbor** — the shipyard where the Elf King Ragnarik built his leviathan
  flagship and a fleet of longships.
- **Citadel of Bear King Thul** — a frozen citadel, seat of the king famed for his
  legions of armoured polar-bear knights.
- **Ruins of Anundshog** — a city of rune-carving mages who *"grew too bold with
  their experiments"* until the last of them destroyed the city in an instant.
- **Tomb of the Light's Champions** — burial place of the magi and Empowered who
  gave their lives to stop the legions of [Queen Hekla](dark-elves.md).
- **Sealed Armoury of the Third Grand War** — where an unspeakable evil, too
  terrible to destroy, was banished into the Void with a weapon forged by Celebrim.
- **The Frost Engine** — the wreck of an attempt to control the weather with Spark
  crystals, which plunged a whole region into an ice age.
- **Wratislaw Magi Academy** — once the most famous school of magic in the world,
  founded by a rare male archmage the equal of any Magi.
- **Light Reach Citadel** — the gleaming-white meeting place of the Great Council of
  High Elf Kings.
- **Matron's Hollow** — the abandoned sacred retreat of the Aeluran Sisters.

## Western Europe

- **The Spire Citadel** — the tower of a legendary oracle, her foresight magnified
  by a treasured *Palantir*.
- **Skip Gates of Camelot** — a ring of portals for instant travel across the earth,
  among the ruins of legendary Camelot.
- **Newgrange Temple** — a holy site where the divide between the worlds of men and
  spirits grows thin.
- **Volcanic Forge of Celebrim** — deep in a volcano, the workshop of Celebrim, the
  greatest smith the elves of Earth ever produced.

## The Mediterranean & Middle East

- **Library of Alexandria** — the greatest repository of elven knowledge the world
  has ever known.
- **Ancient City of Babylon** — seat of the Great Elf Kings of Babylon, whose
  Hanging Gardens were *"a remarkable ode to the Divine Spark."*
- **Apadana Palace** — home to two of history's greatest Elf Lords, Darius the Great
  and his successor Xerxes.
- **Seat of the Storm King** — throne of a Realm Lord so mighty *"the very weather
  obeyed his commands."*
- **Cradle of the First Elves** — one of the first settlements of the original elves
  who came through the portal.
- **Ancient Mega-City of Myropillya** — an elven city said to be as wide and vast as
  a sea; what destroyed it, none know.

## Africa

- **Harkonnen's Foley** — once a paradise, now an endless desert: a ritual to
  harness the land's energy went catastrophically wrong, and colossal serpents are
  said to prowl beneath its sands.
- **The Shattered Spire of Veldric** and **the Grand Blood Altar** — two of the
  great Dark Elven sites. (See [Dark Elves](dark-elves.md).)

## Central & South Asia

- **Heaven's Gate** — a stone complex on the highest peak of the world, where
  nearness to the stars sharpens an oracle's sight.
- **Mythical Spirit Kingdom of Shambhala** — a city most believe never existed,
  where the veil between worlds was all but gone, and its elves were changed by the
  spirits they lived among.
- **Vault of Memories** — caverns of vast Spark crystals into which ancient elves
  wove their very memories, leaving an eternal archive.
- **Ancient City of Mohenjo Daro** — ruins of the Elder Ages, guarded by a storm of
  sand and thunder that erupts from clear air.

## East Asia & the Pacific

- **The Immortal Army of Qin** — where the Emperor Qin bound his soul and his army
  into stone, to wait for a foreseen moment.
- **Mount Huaguo** — the sacred home of **Sun Wukong**, the elven lord and warrior
  known as the Monkey King.
- **Yamata no Orochi's Rest** — where the elven lord **Susanoo** slew the
  eight-headed dragon and claimed the sword *Grass Cutter*.
- **Meng Jiang's Wrath** — where an Aeluran magi's grief, at the murder of her
  husband by a cruel dark elven emperor, shattered a wall, an emperor, and a realm.
- **Ruins of Lemuria** — the remains of the great civilization whose descendants are
  the trickster [Engkanto](races-and-creatures.md).
- **Ruins of Vael'Drakhor** — the Blood Forge, capital of the Dark Elves. (See
  [Dark Elves](dark-elves.md).)

## Legendary figures

The ruins remember a host of heroes and rulers of the ancient world:

| Figure | Remembered for |
|---|---|
| The Last Elf Emperor | Final ruler of the unified empire, betrayed in the Great Rebellion. |
| Celebrim | The greatest elven smith; forged weapons that saved the world. |
| King Ragnarik | Built a leviathan flagship and a legendary longship fleet. |
| Bear King Thul | Commanded knights mounted on armoured polar bears. |
| Ale the Dragon Slayer | Slew the great wyrm Scatha. |
| The Storm King | A Realm Lord whose power commanded the weather. |
| Darius the Great & Xerxes | Two of history's greatest Elf Lords. |
| Emperor Qin | Bound himself and his army into eternal stone. |
| Sun Wukong | The Monkey King — an elven lord and warrior. |
| Susanoo | The elven lord who slew Yamata no Orochi. |
| Meng Jiang | The Aeluran magi whose grief-wrath destroyed an emperor. |

Many of these figures and places will be familiar — Camelot, Babylon, Sun Wukong,
Susanoo. In the world of Elf Destiny they are not borrowed myths but **elven history
that humanity only half-remembers**: the legends of our own world, retold as they
truly were.
