# Nations & Factions

By the start of a campaign, the elves are no longer an empire — they are several
dozen small kingdoms and duchies, scattered across the world and grouped loosely by
the six great [bloodline houses](bloodlines-and-dynasties.md).

## The Valerith realms — the deep north

The largest bloc of elven nations lies in the deep north, and it is the heartland of
elven civilization as it survives today. Chief among its realms is **Deepwood**, the
seat of the [Aeluran Order](aeluran-religion-and-order.md), ruled by Elandra Thalwyn
and governed through an Elven Parliament.

Around it lie the lesser Valerith woods — Blackwood, Mytharil, Queen's Wood, Quiet
Wood, Fell Wood, and the kingdom of **Sylvael**. Two stand out: **Mytharil** is the
world's chief source of *Mythril*, the legendary metal, and **Sylvael** practises
unrestricted consanguinity from the very start — a mark of the Valerith obsession
with the purity of their line.

## The Daelurin realms — the eastern highlands

A martial bloc in the eastern highlands, where the warlike Alfar dwell. At game
start a single ruler, **Ganduin Daelurin**, holds all three of its realms — the
kingdom of **High Hills** and the duchies of **Giants Palm** and **North Wood** — a
traditionalist, war-focused dominion.

## The Thundarael realm — the Crystal Glades

The kingdom of the **Crystal Glades** is the most progressive elven nation in the
world: a realm devoted to magical research, innovation, and scholarship, ruled by
Nictheras Thundarael and home to the most advanced training of Magi anywhere.

## The Serelion realm — the Mediterranean

In the Mediterranean lies **Wyrm's Den**, the realm of Julius Hyperion Serelion — a
naval and martial power that carries the imperial legacy of the elven Roman emperors,
and the cold reputation of the Serelion line.

## The Celtic realms — the Atlantic west

Two realms hold the Atlantic west. **Green Shores**, in the Irish lands, is the
conservative, mystical realm of the Tuatha de Danann under Galanoriel Gwynthorn.
**Atlas**, in Brittany, is the premier seafaring elven nation — explorers,
privateers, and sailors of the Atlareth, led by Aelor Lormelis.

Beyond these, dozens of smaller elven nations dot Europe and the Mediterranean,
elven in culture but outside the great bloodline houses.

## The Aeluran Order

The [Aeluran Order](aeluran-religion-and-order.md) is not a nation but a faction
unto itself — a religious and political power woven through every elven realm. From
its seat at Deepwood it sends advisors into elven courts across the world, guides
the faith, and steers elven politics from behind every throne.

## The choice toward humanity

Every elven realm must, sooner or later, decide how it will regard the humans it
lives among. It is the defining alignment of a campaign:

- **Elven Superiority** — elves are the chosen of the Divine Spark, and humans exist
  to serve. A road of dominion, slave-raiding, and conquest.
- **Open Hearted** — humans and elves alike are children of the Divine Spark, and
  equal. A road of diplomacy, integration, and harmony.

There is no neutral ground. Which path a realm takes shapes everything that follows.
