# Races & Creatures

The world of Elf Destiny holds far more than the six great
[elven cultures](elven-cultures.md). Elven civilisation once spanned the whole
planet and splintered into many peoples, and the world is also home to spirits,
beasts, and monsters — many of them bound up with the magic of the Divine Spark or
loosed from beyond the portal.

## The other elves

The ancient ruins reveal many elven peoples beyond the playable six — most now
extinct, reclusive, or reduced to remnants.

- **The Engkanto** — descendants of the lost archipelago civilisation of **Lemuria**,
  who still guard its stone relics. They are famed as masterful tricksters and
  enchanters, and often dangerous, wicked foes.
- **The Vila** — a reclusive people, believed extinct for millennia, once renowned
  for both their craftsmanship and their unpredictable tempers.
- **The Mrenh Kongveal** — a jungle people no taller than children, vigilant
  protectors of the wild who speak through telepathic whispers and vivid dreams.
  Those who harm their jungle are seized by supernatural terror.
- **Tribal cave elves** — peoples such as the Altamiran, Ajanta, and Saka elves, who
  dwelt in deep cave systems rumoured to connect to a *mystical labyrinth* running
  beneath the whole world.
- **The Elves of the Emerald Vale** — a gentle folk who loved nature deeply, shaping
  homes from living wood and clothing from leaf and flower, before they vanished and
  left only an unsettling presence among their tree-villages.

## Wargs and beasts

**Wargs** are intelligent beast-lords, able to bond with great animals. The clearest
example is **Hwanung**, a warg famed for his loyal dire-bear companion, who once
ruled an elven settlement and woke a volcano to repel a Dark Elven invasion. The
**dire-bears** themselves are intelligent companion-beasts, loyal to the wargs they
serve.

## Dragons and great beasts

- **Greater Drakes** — grand, wingless dragons; the greatest elf warriors once
  tested themselves by hunting them.
- **Scatha** — a great wyrm of the far north, slain by the hero Ale the Dragon
  Slayer.
- **Yamata no Orochi** — a monstrous eight-headed dragon, slain by the elven lord
  Susanoo, who took the sword *Grass Cutter* from its body.
- **Colossal serpents and death worms** — horrors born of magical catastrophe, said
  to prowl beneath the desert sands and to guard the ruins of
  [Vael'Drakhor](dark-elves.md).

## Spirits and otherworldly beings

In many places the veil between the mortal and spirit worlds is thin, and powerful
spirits cross over:

- **Eleke Sazy** — a forest spirit in the form of a mighty stag with antlers beyond
  counting.
- **The golden turtle spirit** of Sword Lake, guardian of a magical blade, who slays
  the unworthy who reach for it.
- **The nine-tailed fox** — the enchantress Daji, who merged with a freed fox spirit
  and became a vengeful tailed woman haunting an enchanted forest.
- **The White Snake** — a powerful elven skinchanger who took the form of a great
  white serpent, devouring men to fuel her power until she fell in love with one of
  her prey, and was finally sealed away by a Zen Spark master of the highest order.
- **Ancestor spirits** — once gentle enough to converse with the living, now grown
  feral and hostile in the long absence of the elves who honoured them.

## Constructs and the undying

The **Immortal Army of Qin** is the strangest legacy of all: the Emperor Qin, warned
by a prophetic dream, bound the souls of himself and his entire army into stone
statues — where they wait still, frozen in eternal vigilance, for a moment only Qin
foresaw. Darker are the **twisted creatures** forged as living weapons by the
bloodmages of [Vael'Drakhor](dark-elves.md).

## Creatures from beyond

Not everything in the world was born in it. The **Ancient Faerie Groves** mark the
site of an old invasion — an unstable portal opened by an elf race from another
realm, spilling out not only armies but much of their world's wildlife and wild
magic, which lingers there yet. And some elves have learned to reach into
[the Void](divine-spark-and-cosmology.md) and summon creatures from the dark space
between worlds — a practice deep in the realm of [dark magic](magic.md).

## A note on giants

There is no separate race of giants in this world. The "giants" of ancient legend
were almost certainly members of [House Daelurin](bloodlines-and-dynasties.md),
whose bloodline breeds elves of gargantuan size.
