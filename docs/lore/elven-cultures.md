# Elven Cultures

Six distinct elven cultures survive into the present day, scattered across the lands
where their peoples weathered the long centuries after the Great Exodus. All belong
to the broader elven family and share a common elven tongue — and each is tied to
one of the six great [bloodline houses](bloodlines-and-dynasties.md).

A trait runs through all of them: elves do not readily abandon their homelands. The
common folk of an elven culture stay rooted where their people have always lived.

## The six cultures

### Deepwood Elven

The heartland culture, and the largest bloc of elves at the start of a campaign.
Settled in the deep north, the Deepwood elves are the people of **House Valerith**
and the seat of the Aeluran Order — a courtly culture that prizes diplomacy,
elegance, and the careful management of bloodlines.

### Alfar

A martial, bellicose culture that values conquest and military glory above all
else. The Alfar are the people of **House Daelurin**, dwelling in the eastern
highlands, with a Scandinavian cast to their ways.

### Romaviri

The heirs of empire — said to descend from elven Roman emperors. The Romaviri are
the people of **House Serelion**, an ambitious, aggressive Mediterranean culture
with a long imperial history behind them.

### Astrion

The most scholarly and magical of the elven cultures, and the most progressive.
Astrion is the culture of **House Thundarael** and their kingdom of the Crystal
Glades, a people devoted to research, learning, and magical advancement.

### Tuatha de Danann

The Celtic-flavoured elves of the Atlantic west, the people of **House Gwynthorn**.
More egalitarian than their kin, they place deep emphasis on family bonds, runestones,
and ancient rites, and feel a kinship with the Irish.

### Atlareth

The maritime culture, settled on the western Atlantic coast — the people of **House
Lormelis** and the premier seafarers among the elves. Their bond with the sea runs
to the bone, and their gaze is fixed on reclaiming the lost glory of Atlantis.

## At a glance

| Culture | House | Region | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deepwood Elven | Valerith | The deep north | Diplomacy and bloodlines |
| Alfar | Daelurin | Eastern highlands | Military conquest |
| Romaviri | Serelion | The Mediterranean | Empire and intrigue |
| Astrion | Thundarael | The Crystal Glades | Magic and scholarship |
| Tuatha de Danann | Gwynthorn | The Atlantic west | Kinship and old rites |
| Atlareth | Lormelis | The western coast | Seafaring and exploration |

## Not the whole of elvenkind

These six are the playable elven cultures — but they are far from all the elves
there are. Ancient elven civilisation spanned the entire world, and it left behind
many other peoples: the trickster Engkanto of lost Lemuria, the long-extinct Vila,
the telepathic Mrenh Kongveal, and assorted tribal and cave-dwelling elves. Those
wider elven kindreds are gathered on the [Races & Creatures](races-and-creatures.md)
page, and the [Dark Elves](dark-elves.md) have a dark history all their own.
