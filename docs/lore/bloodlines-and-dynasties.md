# Bloodlines & Dynasties

Six great elven houses survived the fall of the ancient empire, and each still
carries a **bloodline** of its own — a hereditary gift, always shadowed by a
matching curse, earned through long ages of deliberate breeding. To wed into one of
these houses is to fold its blood, for better and worse, into your own line.

> *Keep an eye out for rulers from ancient elven bloodlines. They carry unique
> genetic traits. Perhaps joining your family with theirs could lead to powerful
> heirs — can you collect them all?*

A bloodline trait is genetic: a child of one bearer has a fair chance of inheriting
it, and a child of two bearers inherits it for certain. (See
[Genetics & Heredity](genetics-and-traits.md).)

## The six great houses

### House Valerith

![House Valerith](../assets/images/ck3/traits/royal_elf_valerith.png)

Silver-haired and ethereally beautiful, the Valerith bred for the flesh until they
could *"coil other lords around their elegant fingers."* But their perfection turns
inward: they suffer a total lack of self-control, growing addicted to indulgence and
falling prey to the very seductions they practise on others. The most purity-obsessed
of the houses — *Pure-blooded* heirs run thick in the Valerith line.

### House Daelurin

![House Daelurin](../assets/images/ck3/traits/royal_elf_daelurin.png)

A house of gargantuan size and martial might. The Daelurin bred for war and
conquest across countless generations, producing lords of such stature that the
**"giants" of ancient legend were almost certainly Daelurin** — there is no separate
race of giants. The cost of that specialisation is a duller mind and a slower step.

### House Serelion

![House Serelion](../assets/images/ck3/traits/royal_elf_serelion.png)

Marked by piercing purple eyes, the Serelion rule through pragmatic cruelty and the
dread they inspire. That dread gives them a fierce grip on their lands — but their
terrified soldiers are seldom truly loyal, and fight poorly when the swords are
drawn. Old lore names the Serelion the descendants of elven Roman emperors.

### House Thundarael

![House Thundarael](../assets/images/ck3/traits/royal_elf_thundarael.png)

Pale-skinned, brilliant, and manic, the Thundarael bred themselves into spark-wielders.
Many of the mightiest Magi and Empowered in all of history came from this house;
their blood lends them great speed and an astonishing pace of learning. But the same
power breeds **madness** — many a Thundarael has been driven to insanity by their
own gift.

### House Gwynthorn

![House Gwynthorn](../assets/images/ck3/traits/royal_elf_gwynthorn.png)

A house of relentless industry and obsessive diligence — tireless caretakers of
their lands and unmatched administrators. Their curse is simpler and sadder than
most: their fixation on duty stresses many Gwynthorns into an early grave.

### House Lormelis

![House Lormelis](../assets/images/ck3/traits/royal_elf_lormelis.png)

Blue-eyed and sea-bound, the Lormelis are revered as unmatched raiders, the
*"descendants of the legendary sea raiders of Atlantis"* — and their driving ambition
is to reclaim that lost glory. Their fierce, single-minded drive is also their
weakness: it leaves them inflexible, blind to changing tides, and prone to strife
within and without.

## Other dynasties

Not every elf of note carries one of the six bloodlines. Elven records name many
lesser houses — among them **Thalwyn**, the line of the ruler of Deepwood;
**Melwood**, the house of the archaeologist who sets the main story in motion; and
**Vaeldrik**, whose name unsettlingly echoes *Vael'Drakhor*, the lost Dark Elven
capital. Important non-bloodline elves are very much a part of the world.

## Myrddin, the Wandering Druid

Some figures resist the neat lines of dynasty altogether. **Myrddin** is one — a
mysterious wandering druid whose heritage is deliberately left in shadow. Raised by a
witch, schooled in druidic ways, he belongs to no house's reckoning. That ambiguity
is the point: Myrddin is meant to be a mystery.

!!! note "What the future may hold"
    Lore points toward a way to **purify** a bloodline — a grand decision for each
    house, earned by achieving some feat that epitomises it, which would strip the
    bloodline of its curse and leave only the gift. This is lore aimed at the mod's
    future rather than its present.
