# Genetics & Heredity

Elven society is built on a single conviction: that excellence is **inherited**, and
that it can be shaped. Who marries whom is never a small matter among elves — it is
the slow, deliberate craft of breeding a better people.

## Inherited gifts

Characters can be born with — or pass down — traits reflecting the physical and
mental qualities of their bloodline. These run in tiers, from the gifted to the
cursed, in three great categories:

- **Beauty** — from the merely *Comely* up to the *Aphroditic*, and down through
  *Homely* to the *Hideous*. Rare and exotic variants exist for the truly unusual.
- **Physique** — bodily strength and vigour, from the *Hale* to the *Titanic*, or
  down to the *Feeble*.
- **Intellect** — sharpness of mind, from the *Quick* to the *Erudite*, or down to
  the *Imbecile*.

Pair two parents who each carry a gift, and their children have a better chance of
inheriting it — and of inheriting it *stronger*. Generation by generation, a
disciplined house can concentrate beauty, brawn, or brilliance into its line.

## Blood purity

Beyond the universal gifts lie traits of **blood purity** — *Pure-blooded*,
*Purer-blooded*, *Purest-blooded* — which appear only when both parents belong to
the same dynasty. They are the reward for keeping a bloodline tightly woven, and
they are exactly what elven nobility strives for: an heir of the highest purity.

The opposite danger is the **Inbred** trait — the penalty of pushing close-family
marriage too far, too fast. Purity and ruin lie along the same road.

This is why elven cultures embrace what other peoples forbid. Close marriage is no
taboo when the goal is a perfect line, and the practice is woven into elven law and
the matchmaking of the [Aeluran Order](aeluran-religion-and-order.md) alike.

## Bloodlines of the great houses

Separate from these universal gifts are the **bloodline traits** of the six great
ancient dynasties — Valerith, Serelion, Gwynthorn, Thundarael, Daelurin, and
Lormelis. Each is the unique inheritance of one house, a signature gift bound to a
matching curse. They are covered on their own page:
[Bloodlines & Dynasties](bloodlines-and-dynasties.md).

## What is *not* inherited

For all the elven faith in blood, one thing does **not** pass through it:
**immortality**. An elf's deathlessness is not carried in the bloodline — it is
granted according to their [Ascension tier](elven-ascension.md). The *elf* itself is
heritable; the immortality is a consequence of how high that elf has climbed.
