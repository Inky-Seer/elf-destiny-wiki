# Magic

The magic of the elves is called **Spark Weaving** — the art of shaping *Spark*, the
life energy created by the [Divine Spark](divine-spark-and-cosmology.md), into
patterns of power. The higher an elf rises through the
[Ascension](elven-ascension.md) tiers, the deeper their natural attunement to the
Spark, and the greater their magic.

## How men and women weave

Elves wield the Spark differently depending on their sex:

- **Women** become **Magi** — battle spellcasters who can blast their enemies apart
  with devastating magic.
- **Men** become **Empowered Knights** — warriors whose bodies are flooded with
  Spark energy until they fight as superhuman soldiers.

This is the deep reason the [Aeluran Order](aeluran-religion-and-order.md) is an
all-female sisterhood: the Order trains the world's most powerful Magi, and that
particular gift — raw spellcasting — belongs to women.

## The levels of Spark Weaving

Formal magical practice advances through a series of stages, each a deeper mastery
of the Spark:

| Stage | Mastery |
|---|---|
| **Refined Spark Weaving** | Weaving Spark into intricate, deliberate patterns of power — the foundation of all formal magic. |
| **Crystalic Amplification** | Storing and channelling Spark through crystals. |
| **Ambient Spark Harvesting** | Drawing power directly from the fabric of reality itself. |
| **Archonic Rituals** | Tapping the immense power of the ancient Archons. |
| **Divine Avatars** | Manifesting avatars that channel godly power into the world. |

Each stage builds on the last, carrying the weaver closer to the kind of power the
Valar command.

## Crystal magic

Spark can be stored in **crystal**, and this makes crystals both weapon and
treasure. As batteries, they amplify spells and serve as reservoirs of power. But
they can hold more than raw energy: at the **Vault of Memories**, ancient elves of
great wisdom wove their very memories into enormous Spark crystals, leaving an
eternal archive of their knowledge. Spark crystals are, at once, batteries and
libraries.

They are also dangerous. The disaster of the **Frost Engine** began as an attempt to
harness Spark crystals to control the weather — and ended by plunging an entire
region into an age of ice.

## Oracles and prophecy

Prophecy is a recognised branch of Spark magic. **Oracles** seek out places where
Spark runs thick to sharpen their visions — none more so than **Heaven's Gate**, set
on the highest peak of the world, where the concentration of Spark grants clearer
and more potent foresight. The legendary oracle of the **Spire Citadel** saw further
still, her sight magnified by a treasured *Palantir*, a crystal seeing-stone.

Prophetic dreams shape the world's history: it was such a dream that drove the
ancient Emperor Qin to bind his own soul, and his entire army, into eternal stone.

## Dark magic

The Spark can be turned to terrible ends. The clearest example is the **blood
magic** of the Dark Elves — mass sacrifice to forge dread "blood stones", and the
crafting of twisted creatures as living weapons of war.

!!! note "What the future may hold"
    Elven lore speaks of darker arts still — Void Summoners who call creatures from
    the [Void](divine-spark-and-cosmology.md), blood magicians, and those who Ascend
    "the immoral way". This is lore that points toward the mod's future rather than
    its present.

## Magi among the elves

Two kinds of weaver stand out in the elven world. The **Aeluran Sisters**, trained
from youth, are the most disciplined Magi of all — and their Matrons more gifted
still. And the **High Magi** — few in number but devastating in war — are the elven
spellcasters who turn a battlefield with a handful of figures rather than a host.
