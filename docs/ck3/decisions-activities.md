# Decisions & Activities

Beyond [expeditions](expeditions.md), Elf Destiny adds a set of decisions and
activities for running an elven realm — from the rituals that drive Ascension to the
Aeluran Order arranging your family's marriages.

## Religious rituals

The mod's most important decisions are its **rituals**, taken through the Aeluran
faith.

- **Ascension Ritual** — the core path of elven [Ascension](elf-races-traits.md). It
  is available to any elf below the top tier, costs **piety**, and invokes the
  Divine Spark; if the Spark is receptive, your character emerges a higher being.
  Crossing a major threshold (into the Fae, Celestial, or Ainur tiers) uses a
  dedicated **threshold ritual**.
- **Blessing Ritual** — petition the Divine Spark for one of its [Blessings](cultures-religion.md#blessings).

## Realm decisions

| Decision | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Adopt Elven Elective** | Adopts the [Elven Elective](government-diarchies.md#succession-elven-elective) succession law on your primary title. Available to landed elf rulers of county tier or higher. |
| **Adopt Ascended Tribal Government** | Switches you — and your vassals — to the [Ascended Tribal](government-diarchies.md#ascended-tribal) government. Requires the Tribal Ascension tradition, costs **1000 prestige and 400 gold**, and converts a holding in every county of your realm to a tribal holding. |
| **The Wandering** | Configures the *Wandering* — the custom that draws the eldest elves away into the wilds or through the Grand Portal. Wanderers are treated as departed, freeing their titles for heirs, which keeps long-lived elven realms from stagnating. The setting can be enabled or disabled. |

## Personal decisions

| Decision | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tame a Direwolf Pup** | If a direwolf pack lives in your capital county, attempt to claim a pup as a pet. Costs 500 prestige, with a 5-year cooldown — and it is dangerous: a strong physique helps, the *Warg* trait helps greatly, and a failed attempt can kill you. |
| **Break an Entrancement** | Attempt to free yourself from an Enchantress's *Entrance* (see [Schemes, Hooks & Contracts](schemes-hooks-contracts.md)). |
| **Visit the Peddler's Shop** | Once a black-market peddler has handed you his business card, seek out his elusive shop in the market. |

## Activities

Elf Destiny adds its own activities alongside CK3's feasts and hunts:

- **[Expeditions](expeditions.md)** — the central activity: exploring ancient elven
  ruins. Covered on its own page.
- **Aeluran Matchmaking** — the Aeluran Order considers it their duty to see elf
  lords well married. In this activity a **Red Sister** curates a slate of spousal
  candidates for you or a family member, chosen for bloodline quality, and presents
  the match. It is the order's instrument for steering elven dynasties toward
  [bloodline](bloodlines.md) purity.
- **The Entrance Feast** — a feast tied to the *Entrance* power, where an elf turns
  the gathering's social currents to their advantage.

!!! note "Story decisions"
    Some major decisions — *What is an Archaeologist?* and *Transport the Grand
    Portal* among them — drive the main story rather than ordinary play. Those are
    covered on the [Story Events](story-events.md) page.
