# Crusader Kings III Mod

**Elf Destiny** for Crusader Kings III is the original, fuller version of the mod —
a fantasy overhaul that drops elves into CK3's medieval world and hands you the job
of bringing their lost realm back.

## The premise

You begin as an ordinary human ruler. An archaeologist arrives at your court with
word of strange ancient ruins, and once you investigate them everything changes:
you are transformed into an elf, and marked as the **Realm Lord** — the one destined
to lead elvenkind's return to power.

From there the campaign is about rediscovering what the elves lost: their long
lifespans and god-like potential, their forgotten culture, their faith, and the
ancient [Grand Portal](../lore/portal-network.md) that once linked this world to
countless others.

## Getting started

1. **Subscribe** to the mod on the
   [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3114064450).
2. In the CK3 launcher, add Elf Destiny to a playset and enable it. It is a large
   overhaul — if you run other major mods, check the [Discord](https://discord.gg/fyuPcf5HFZ)
   for compatibility notes first.
3. Start a normal campaign as any human ruler. Soon after the game begins, an
   **archaeologist** visits your court — accept, and follow the expedition that
   discovers the ruins. Exploring them triggers your transformation into an elf.

That first ruin is the gateway to everything else: Ascension, culture, the Aeluran
faith, and the long quest to restore the Grand Portal.

!!! info "Beta software"
    Elf Destiny is an actively developed beta by a solo creator. Built for Crusader
    Kings III **1.19.x**. Expect frequent updates — and the occasional bug. Bug
    reports are very welcome on the Discord.
