# Expeditions

Expeditions are the beating heart of Elf Destiny. They are the activity through
which you explore the ancient elven ruins scattered across the world — and almost
everything the mod adds, from your own elfhood to lost cultural traditions, is
discovered out there in the ruins.

> *Expeditions are about rediscovering the secrets of the ancient Elven peoples. You
> and an entourage of knights and adventurers will explore Elven ruins in search of
> long lost knowledge and great treasures.*

## Where it begins

Your very first expedition is the doorway into the whole mod. Any ruler can take the
one-time major decision **"What is an Archaeologist?"**, which sets the story in
motion: an archaeologist brings word of strange ancient ruins, and exploring that
first site is what transforms your character into an [elf](elf-races-traits.md).

From then on, expeditions become a repeatable part of your reign.

## Hosting an expedition

An expedition is an **activity**, hosted much like a feast or a hunt — but it can
only be held at an **expedition site**, and only at sites you have already
*discovered*. More sites reveal themselves as the story and your explorations
progress.

Hosting one costs **gold** (scaled to the game era) and has a cooldown before you
can mount another. When you plan it, you choose two things:

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Funding** | Minimal (2 weeks) · Average (3 weeks) · Extravagant (5 weeks) — longer funding means a more thorough search of the ruins. |
| **Party size** | Small Group (3) · Decent Group (5) · Large Entourage (7) adventurers. |

You set out with an entourage of knights and adventurers, and may invite family and
courtiers along.

## Inside the ruins

Once the party arrives, the expedition plays out through a **Base Camp** phase: the
adventurers press into the ruins and face whatever waits there — trials, traps,
riddles, and unique encounters.

It is genuinely dangerous. Participants can come home with **adventurer experience**,
improved **opinion** of their fellow explorers, **prestige**, and relief from
**stress** — but they can also be **injured or killed**. The ancient sites do not
give up their secrets for free.

## Rewards

A successful expedition can yield:

- **Gold** and **ancient Elven artifacts**.
- A newly discovered **cultural tradition** — one of the Lost Traditions. This is how
  powerful unlocks such as the [Ascended Tribal](government-diarchies.md#ascended-tribal)
  government enter your game.
- **Transformation** — expedition party members can themselves be turned into elves.

Because tradition discoveries are partly down to luck, expeditions are something you
return to again and again: each one is another roll at the secrets of the elven
past.

## The expedition sites

The world holds dozens of unique, hand-written expedition sites, each with its own
history — fallen empires, dark elf strongholds, oracle spires, drowned cities, and
the Grand Portal itself. Many recast real-world myth and history as elven legend.

??? example "A sample of the sites"
    - **The Grand Portal** — the world-gate through which elves and humans first
      arrived, ringed by a stone elven city of the First Age.
    - **Ruins of Valfuria** — a once-mighty Elf Empire capital, much of it eerily
      intact, and something sinister still prowling its streets.
    - **The Spire Citadel** — the tower of a legendary oracle and her treasured
      Palantir.
    - **Library of Alexandria** — the greatest repository of elven knowledge the
      world has ever known.
    - **Volcanic Forge of Celebrim** — the workshop of the greatest smith the elves
      of Earth ever produced.
    - **Ruins of Vael'Drakhor** — the Blood Forge, ancient capital of the Dark
      Elves, still guarded by venom-spraying death worms.
    - **Mount Huaguo** — the sacred mountain home of Sun Wukong, the elven Monkey
      King.

    ...and many more, from the Tomb of the Last Emperor to Heaven's Gate.
