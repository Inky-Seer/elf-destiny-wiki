# Buildings

Elf Destiny reworks parts of CK3's holding economy for elves — extra tiers of tribal
buildings for risen tribes, a cultural farm that thrives in the deep woods, and
special buildings at the faith's holy sites.

## Tribal buildings, reimagined

A realm that has taken the [Ascended Tribal](government-diarchies.md#ascended-tribal)
government can build **two extra levels of every tribal building** — levels that
ordinary tribes can never reach. At those upper tiers the buildings shed their plain
names for something far grander, raised with elven craft and strengthened by the
Divine Spark itself:

| Tribal building | Elven tier 3 | Elven tier 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Tribal Hold | Elven Hold | Elven Grand Hold |
| Longhouses | Halls of the Great Feast | Hero's Hall |
| War Camps | Champions Crucible | Hero's Foundry |
| Palisades | Natural Fortifications | Citadel of the Wilds |
| Market Villages | Commerce Shaman Hall | Auction House and Trade Center |

The fortification tiers are the headline: an *Elven Grand Hold* or *Citadel of the
Wilds* is said to stand stronger than any human castle — a tribe that fortifies
without ever becoming feudal.

## Woodland Grove Farms

Cultures with the **Woodland Bounty** [tradition](cultures-religion.md#the-elven-traditions)
can raise **Woodland Grove Farms** — fields of unusual crops bred to flourish in
forest and taiga, terrain where ordinary farms cannot be built at all. It lets an
elven realm turn its deep woods into productive farmland rather than clearing them.

## Special buildings

A handful of unique special buildings anchor key locations:

- **Deep Wood Sanctum** — a university where the sisters of the Aeluran Order are
  trained. Its doors open only to promising women, drawing the brightest minds of
  the realm to study the arcane arts.
- **Ancient Portal Ruins** — the special building at the Grand Portal holy site:
  massive scattered stone carvings and a great half-crescent of rock, the remnant of
  a gateway to another world. It is the seed from which the
  [restored Grand Portal](story-events.md) grows.
- **Grand Temple** — the special building of the faith's central holy site.

The faith's many other holy sites are the ancient elven ruins themselves — the
places you visit on [expeditions](expeditions.md).
