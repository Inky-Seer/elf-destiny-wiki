# Story Events

Elf Destiny has a hand-written main questline running through a campaign. It begins
the moment you take the *What is an Archaeologist?* decision and carries you from
ordinary human ruler to **Realm Lord** of a restored Grand Portal.

!!! warning "This page spoils the main story"
    Everything below describes the mod's central questline. If you would rather
    discover it in play, stop here — the [Overview](index.md) and other pages cover
    the mechanics without the plot.

## The questline at a glance

The story so far forms **Act 1**, told in a series of major decisions and the events
they trigger. Each stage is collapsed below — open them in order.

??? note "1 — The archaeologist"
    Take the one-time major decision **"What is an Archaeologist?"** when a mysterious
    woman arrives at your court seeking an audience.

    She is **Julia Melwood**, an archaeologist who studies the lost Elven
    civilisation. She brings a map covered in undecipherable Elven script that may
    show the **fortress tomb of the Last Elf Emperor** — and asks you to fund an
    expedition to find it.

??? note "2 — Becoming an elf"
    Agreeing unlocks the [Expeditions](expeditions.md) activity. Your first
    expedition into the ancient ruins is the turning point of the campaign: your
    character is **transformed into an elf**, and you recover the **Sigil of the
    Realm Lord** — an artifact that marks you as the one destined to lead elvenkind's
    return.

??? note "3 — The Aeluran Order"
    With the Sigil in hand, you make contact with the **Aeluran Order**, the
    sisterhood that has kept the elven faith alive. This conversation opens the rest
    of the questline — and the path to weaving the order into your
    [government](government-diarchies.md).

??? note "4 — Transporting the Grand Portal"
    The decision **"Transport the Grand Portal"** becomes available. The portal's
    enormous stone pieces are loaded onto wagons and hauled, slowly, to your
    **capital** — *"The New Age will begin there."*

??? note "5 — Gathering the components"
    A restored portal needs more than its stones. Further expeditions must recover
    its two lost working parts — the **Portal Capacitor** and the **Navigation
    Relay** — as artifacts.

??? note "6 — Restoring the Grand Portal"
    With both components and the Sigil, take **"Restore the Grand Portal."**

    > *"After many trials and great personal cost the Grand Portal is finally
    > restored! Once more the gateway between this world and the countless realms of
    > the galaxy is open... The time of Elves comes again!"*

    You are now a **true Realm Lord**. This concludes the currently implemented main
    story — *Act 1* — and the mod tells you so directly: more is under construction.

## A side thread — Reforming the Aeluran Faith

Alongside the portal quest runs the decision **"Reform the Aeluran Faith."** The
Grand Matriarch sets you a test: rediscover at least three ancient elven holy sites
that still resonate with the Divine Spark. Pass it, and the Order will defer parts
of the Aeluran religion to your judgement — letting a Realm Lord imprint their own
will on a faith that has stood unchanged for a thousand years.
