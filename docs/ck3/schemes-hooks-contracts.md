# Schemes, Hooks & Contracts

Elf Destiny adds an elven scheme — *Entrance* — along with the hooks it produces and
a special set of vassal contracts for the Aeluran Order.

## The Entrance scheme

**Entrance** is a secret personal scheme, unlocked for cultures with the
[Beguiling Nature](cultures-religion.md#blessings) tradition. An elf uses their power
over the Divine Spark to dominate another character's will and bring them under
control.

- It is an **intrigue** scheme targeting a single character, takes roughly a year to
  build, and resolves somewhere between a 5% and 95% chance of success.
- The success chance turns on **how much stronger you are** than your target — the
  difference in [Elf tier](elf-races-traits.md#the-ascension-tiers), your talent at
  manipulating the Spark, and the gap in learning and intrigue skill.
- On success, the target gains the [*Entranced*](elf-races-traits.md#notable-traits)
  trait and you gain a **perpetual hook** over them.

Entrancement has depth. Each time you press your advantage, the victim's
entrancement deepens; once it runs deep enough, *Entranced* gives way to
*Enthralled* — a stronger, more complete hold. Both traits quietly sap the victim's
learning and personal growth.

A character caught in an Entrance is not without recourse: the **Break an
Entrancement** decision (see [Decisions & Activities](decisions-activities.md#personal-decisions))
lets them struggle to throw off the influence. Beguiling Nature also lets an elf
bring both **Intrigue and Diplomacy** to bear in a Battle of Wills.

## Hooks

The mod adds custom hook types beyond CK3's defaults:

| Hook | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Entranced** | Perpetual — the hold gained over a character through the Entrance scheme. |
| **Enthralled** | Perpetual and *strong* — the deeper hold over a character whose entrancement has fully matured. |
| **Religious Exaltation** | A temporary hook (about ten years) granted through the faith. |
| **Matchmaking Favoritism** | A temporary hook (about five years), earned through Aeluran matchmaking. |
| **Overlooked Infraction** | A temporary hook (about five years). |
| **Forgiven Traitor** | Perpetual and strong — held over a traitor whose treason you have formally pardoned. |

## Aeluran vassal contracts

Vassals of the [Aeluran Order](government-diarchies.md#the-aeluran-regency) do not
serve under ordinary feudal contracts. They have their own contract with three
obligation tracks, and **which levels you are allowed to set is governed by the
Aeluran Regency's balance of power** — the more the order respects you, the harder
you can push.

Each track runs through five steps — *Exempt, Low, Normal, High, Extortionate*:

- **Taxes** — how much gold the Aeluran vassal owes. Heavier taxes fill your coffers
  but cost you their goodwill; lighter ones leave them free to develop their own
  church holdings.
- **Levies** — how many troops they raise for you, from none up to half their
  strength.
- **Magi** — the distinctive elven track: how many **Magi** the vassal contributes.
  Demand little and the vassal keeps a larger personal corps of spark-wielders;
  demand much and those Magi are pulled into your service instead.

Pushing every track to *Extortionate* squeezes the most out of an Aeluran vassal —
at a steep cost to their opinion of you — so the contracts are best read alongside
the regency's [balance of power](government-diarchies.md#the-aeluran-regency).
