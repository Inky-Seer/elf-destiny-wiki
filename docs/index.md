# Elf Destiny Wiki

Welcome to the community wiki for **Elf Destiny** — an alternative-history fantasy
elf total-conversion mod, available for both **Crusader Kings III** and
**Europa Universalis V**.

This wiki has three parts:

- **[Lore](lore/index.md)** — the shared world, history, and mythology behind both mods.
- **[CK3 Mod](ck3/index.md)** — gameplay mechanics for the Crusader Kings III version.
- **[EU5 Mod](eu5/index.md)** — gameplay mechanics for the Europa Universalis V version.

    This wiki is being built. Some sections are placeholders for now — check back as
    pages are filled in.
